Wayne is a free, great looking DRM-free comic book reader and library manager for iPhone and iPad. Roadmap: [rubengarciam/wayne](https://github.com/rubengarciam/wayne)

## Your complete library in one place

Forget manually importing what you want to read, one at a time. Just log in with your cloud storage account and select where you keep your comics to start organizing your library. It's that simple.

Wayne supports iCloud Drive and Dropbox. Other cloud storage solutions are on its way. For more information check the roadmap link bellow.

## Rich metadata

Retrieve any comic's metadata from Goodreads or manually edit it yourself. Add your comics to collections, series and story arcs to keep things organized the way you want.

![wayne library image](/assets/img/projects/wayne.metadata.png)

## Grids, lists and more

Display your library in a cover grid, detailed lists or grouped by metadata properties. You can also search or filter by specific information like reading status, favorites, publishers and others.

![wayne hero image](/assets/img/projects/wayne.library.png)

## Read on the go

Save comics offline and read them wherever you are. Wayne also keeps track of your reading progress and supports right-to-left reading.

![wayne read image](/assets/img/projects/wayne.read.png)

## Everything is in sync

No need to sign up or creating a profile. Your library is automatically synchronized across multiple devices with your iCloud account out of the box.

## Your stuff is yours only

There are no ads or analytics that track what you do. The contents of your library are yours and private, as it should be.

## Free

The goal is to keep Wayne free, with an in-app donation system if you think it's worth it and want to help covering maintenance costs.
